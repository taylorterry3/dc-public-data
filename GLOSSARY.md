# DC public data

Public incident data and the reviewed updates maintained by this project.

## Language

**Annual incident dataset**:
A year-specific crime-incident dataset published through DC Open Data. It is a separate source from the Crime Cards export.

**Crime Cards export**:
An incident-data export from DC's Crime Cards application. Its coverage and publication timing must be assessed separately from the annual incident datasets.

**Accepted data**:
The reviewed version of a dataset on the repository's main branch. Downloaded or proposed updates remain candidates until reviewed and merged.

**Observed record**:
A source occurrence seen in a complete annual download. Multiple occurrences may share a complaint number; publisher row identifiers do not establish continuity across downloads.

**Retained record**:
One occurrence maintained across observations, including when absent from the latest complete source snapshot. It has one current version and may have superseded versions.

**First seen on / last seen on**:
The first and most recent dates a successful refresh observed a retained record in the source. Unknown historical dates remain unknown; an unexamined year does not advance either date.

**Superseded version**:
Previously observed values of a retained record, preserved when a correction replaces its current values. It is history, not an additional incident.
